Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

SwoReel collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Your reels stay on your computer.

This policy covers SwoReel, made by Desoto Labs LLC: the SwoReel plugin for Claude Code, the SwoReel desktop app, and this website, sworeel.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Desoto Labs LLC does not receive, store or see your reels, your exports, your settings, your conversations with Claude, or anything else about you or your computer. There is no account to create, and nothing is sent to us.

Where your reels are kept

Reels, the pictures they use, your settings and your exports are ordinary files on your own computer. The plugin's sworeel paths command shows exactly where. They stay there until you delete them, and moving or deleting them is up to you.

What SwoReel connects to

SwoReel works on your computer and makes no network requests of its own, with these exceptions, each to a public service run by someone else:

  • Installing the plugin downloads its software dependencies, once, from the public npm registry (registry.npmjs.org), run by GitHub.
  • The desktop app checks GitHub for a newer version of itself when it starts and every few hours while it is open, and downloads it from GitHub when there is one.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and asks GitHub for the newest release so that its download buttons point at it.

Like any website or download, these services can see the request itself, such as your IP address. What they do with it is set out in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive any of it.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Claude

SwoReel works inside Claude Code, and you make reels by talking to Claude. Your conversations, and anything you ask Claude to read, such as a web page, a file or a repository, are handled by Claude Code and Anthropic under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. SwoReel does not receive them and sends nothing from them anywhere.

Children

SwoReel is not directed at children under 13, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. SwoReel's source code is public, so how it works can always be checked.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about SwoReel can be asked on SwoReel's issues page on GitHub.
